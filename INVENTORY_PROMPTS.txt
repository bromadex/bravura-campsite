INVENTORY MANAGEMENT (IN) MODULE — IMPLEMENTATION PLAN  (v2, 2026-07-13)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Module code: IN   |   T-codes: IN01+   |   Color: #B45309 (amber-brown, TBD)
Permission gates: NEW inventory.view / create / edit / delete / approve

VISION
Inventory is the next foundational module after HR and Fuel — built before
Finance, because Procurement, Fuel, Fleet, Maintenance and eventually Finance
all depend on it. It is the central source of truth for every physical item
the company owns or consumes: electrical materials, mechanical spares, civil
materials, PPE, stationery, consumables, lubricants, chemicals, mining
consumables, IT equipment, kitchen supplies — with fuel as a summary
integration and fixed assets handed to a future Assets module.
Every movement is traceable from purchase to consumption.

ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS (agreed)
  1. INVENTORY ≠ PROCUREMENT. Two domains from day one. Inventory answers
     "what do we have, where, who used it". Procurement answers "what do we
     buy, from whom, at what price". Procurement screens live in the existing
     PR module (Suppliers already built there) and FEED inventory via GRN.
  2. CAMPSITE SUPPLIES STAYS IN CAMPSITE. Not absorbed. It becomes an
     integration client of Inventory like Fleet/Meals/HR (camp store can be
     modelled as a warehouse; camp supplies can be issued from central stock).
  3. LEDGER ARCHITECTURE (adopted from old ERP — its best idea):
     quantities live in a per-item-per-warehouse balances table
     (UNIQUE(item_id, warehouse_id)) and EVERY movement is an immutable
     `inventory_movements` row (signed qty, running balance, valuation rate).
     Pages never write balances directly — a trigger maintains balances and
     moving-average cost from movements. AI-ready by construction.
  4. Valuation: moving average cost (AVCO). No FIFO layers.
  5. Negative stock blocked at issue time (override needs inventory.approve).
  6. Warehouses are site-scoped (warehouses.site_id) — RLS and the site
     switcher work exactly like every other module; HQ sees all sites.

ADOPTED FROM OLD ERP (bravuraerpmain_4.zip, 28-page module — take the good,
skip the enterprise bloat):
  ADOPT: bins/ledger data model, Stock Balance (home screen), Stock In (GRN),
         controlled Stock Out, Transfers with status flow
         (draft→pending→approved→in transit→received), Warehouses, Categories,
         Item Shortage/Reorder view, Opening Stock, Stock Taking with
         variance + approval, Stock Ledger report, Batch Expiry (kitchen,
         Phase 4), UOM conversion (only if bulk-buy/split-issue proves needed)
  SKIP:  Pick Lists, Stock Reservations, Consignment, Putaway Rules,
         Bin/Aisle/Rack/Shelf locations (a simple free-text `location` on the
         item is enough for our store sizes), Item Variants, Serial tracking
         + repair logs, Cycle Count (Stock Taking covers it), AVCO
         balance-sheet valuation report (simple value column instead), FIFO
         ageing, Forecast engine + Auto-MR scheduler (reorder list + one-click
         requisition beats both at our scale), duplicate Transactions page
